GET TO KNOW ME

About Me

I was born on April 24, 2011, in Bahir Dar, Ethiopia, where I have lived my entire life. My educational journey began at Bahir Dar Academy (BDA), where I attended kindergarten. Even before officially starting school, I was a little girl who was eager to learn. I still remember how excited I felt when my parents bought me my first school bag. I carried it around all day because I was so happy about the idea of going to school. During my free time, I enjoyed drawing and reading books. These activities helped me explore my creativity and develop my curiosity. After completing Grade 5, I transferred to SOS Hermann Gmeiner School, where I continued my education until Grade 9. I am currently studying at STEM, hoping to complete my high school education here. Being a freshman has not always been easy. I have faced different challenges, but I always try my best to handle them and find solutions independently. Every experience teaches me something new and helps me grow both academically and personally. This is only the beginning of my journey, and I look forward to discovering what the future holds.

LOOKING AHEAD

My Goals

One of my biggest ambitions is to become a business owner in a field I am passionate about. Although I prefer to keep the details of my future plans private, I actively work toward this ambition by organizing my days, observing the world around me, and thinking about possible business ideas based on the problems I notice in my community. Someone who has inspired me over the past few years is Dr. Feven Birara, a resident doctor specializing in plastic, reconstructive, and aesthetic surgery in China, as well as a business owner associated with Birara General Trading and a part-time model.Her ambition, commitment, and determination motivate me to work toward becoming someone who can balance different passions and responsibilities. Whenever I think about her journey, I feel encouraged to keep working toward my own.
