About Me

to start, I was born on April 24, 2011, in Bahir Dar, Ethiopia, and I have lived here my entire life.I began my educational journey at Bahir Dar Academy (BDA), where I attended kindergarten. Even before I officially started school, I was a little girl who was eager to learn. I remember how excited I was when my parents bought me a school bag. I carried it around all day because it made me feel so happy and excited about going to school.During my free time, I enjoyed drawing and reading books. & these activities allowed me to explore my creativity and develop my curiosity.After completing Grade 5, I transferred to SOS Hermann Gmeiner School, where I continued my education until Grade 9. Currently, I am a student at STEM, hoping to complete my high school here.Being a freshman has not always been easy. I have faced many difficulties along the way. However, I always try my best to face these challenges and find solutions on my own. Every experience teaches me something new and helps me grow as a student and as a person.This is only the beginning of my journey, and I look forward to discovering what the future holds.
